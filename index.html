    How your choices compare

    "Give it everything" scores below "Give it nothing" and sends the most tokens: bad inputs do more damage than none, and you pay for them.

    What I'd tell my team

    1. 1
      Relevance beats volumeEvery bad input costs more than a good one adds. Bigger context windows don't fix this: longer input costs more and gets less reliable, even when every piece is good.
    2. 2
      Check the source and the dateThe old reorder policy had a 0.89 match score. A high match can still be outdated, for the wrong audience, or unsafe.

      Mistakes compoundA bad design input becomes a bad spec, then bad code. Check the agent's inputs between every step, not only at the end.
