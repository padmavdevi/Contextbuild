A 3-minute game

Context Triage

An AI agent is building a sales inventory dashboard for a retail chain in three steps: design, spec, then code. Before each step you choose what information the agent gets. Some of it helps. Some of it looks helpful and isn't.

1. Read the taskEach round is one step of the same build.
2. Pick the cardsTap the cards you want the agent to use. Check the source and the age, not just the match score.
3. See what it buildsBad cards make the agent build the wrong thing. Missing good cards make it guess.

    How your choices compare

    "Give it everything" scores below "Give it nothing": bad information does more damage than no information.

    What to take away

    Relevance beats volumeMore information isn't better information. Every bad card you let in costs more than a good card adds.
    Check the source and the dateThe old reorder policy had a 0.89 match score. A high match can still be outdated, from the wrong audience, or unsafe.
    Mistakes compoundA bad design input becomes a bad spec, then bad code. Check the agent's inputs between every step, not only at the end.
